MOLIBRA

public chain · native coin MOLI · chain id 20226

A chain that records what people chose to do, not what they said when asked. Preference revealed by action is the datum; the record is a record of acts. No privileged verifier, open entry, and equal weight enforced by the protocol rather than promised in a document.

Start here

You need no money to begin. Chalk is earned by solving a puzzle bound to your own address, so a wallet holding nothing can still speak.

Connect a wallet
Add Molibra to MetaMask, or make a key in the page. No seed phrase, no faucet, no exchange.
/molibra/connect
Earn chalk
Solve a puzzle bound to your address and receive the units expression costs. Hands over no MOLI.
/molibra/chalk
Create a token
A question or an asset. The id is derived, never chosen, so nobody squats a name.
/molibra/create

Markets

Any pair in two hops, one pool per token, and expression tokens structurally excluded — a question has no market because it has no price.

DEX
Swap any listed pair, route through two hops, deploy the factory and open a market from the page.
/molibra/swap
Liquidity
Add and read depth. A deposit adds depth; it never sets a price.
/molibra/pool
Chart
Price and depth over time, drawn from the chain's own blocks.
/molibra/chart
Broker
The Ethereum side: WSRO, WETH and the live position that holds the only real liquidity.
/molibra/broker

Bridge to Ethereum

One way, and honest about it. Burning MOLI destroys it; there is no instruction that brings it back. Backing is a bonded attestation, not proof of work.

bMOLI
Destroy MOLI here, mint the same amount on Ethereum against a proved burn.
/molibra/bridgedmoli
Settlement
Anchor a block on Ethereum and release a bridge-out against the proof.
/molibra/settle
Inbound
Assets arriving from another chain. Expression never crosses, in any wrapping.
/molibra/inbound
Bridge out
Sign the statement that a holding is leaving. It destroys nothing on its own.
/molibra/bridge
