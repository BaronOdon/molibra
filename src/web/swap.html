04 · factory

One pool per token, created by anyone, once. Every pool has MOLI on one side, which is what makes any pair reachable in two hops.

⛔⛔ An expression token can never get a market. GIZ and anything declaring a social, purchase or electoral purpose live in the chain's registry and are not contracts — there is no bytecode to pool. The factory refuses them structurally, not by a rule it could forget.
