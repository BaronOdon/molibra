Molibra chain 20226

MOLI against a bridged asset, in MolibraPool. MOLI is the native coin, so it goes in as the transaction's value — there is no wrapper.

⛔⛔ A token you mint here is NOT bridged WSRO. Until the bridge runs, anything you deploy and mint on this side is a stand-in you created. Pairing MOLI against it prices MOLI against your own token, not against WSRO. Real bridged units arrive only by burning WSRO on Ethereum and proving that burn — that is what minted here == proved burns there − returns means.
The first deposit sets the price here too, and permanently burns 1000 shares so the pool can never be drained and re-founded.

Anchor Molibra → Ethereum

Publish a Molibra state commitment into Ethereum. Once anchored, chain.js refuses any branch forking below that height however much work it carries — so rewriting Molibra history stops being a mining problem and becomes an Ethereum reorg problem.

⛔ Bonding in WSRO is only safe because mint is renounced — a publisher who could re-mint its own bond makes slashing theatre. Verified before this panel was written.
