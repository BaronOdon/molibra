Bridge test

Prove a Molibra transaction to a contract on your Ethereum network, and release ETH against it. Your wallet signs everything; this page holds no key and the Molibra node holds none of your ETH.

⛔ This is a test rig, not a bridge. It releases ETH against a single Molibra header with valid proof of work — and Molibra's difficulty is small enough that such a header can be mined privately in seconds. Anyone who can do that can drain whatever you put in it. Fund it with an amount you are willing to lose, on a network you do not mind. A real bridge needs a header chain with accumulated difficulty and a confirmation depth, and — before it moves anybody else's value — the written legal opinions this project's compliance file requires.
