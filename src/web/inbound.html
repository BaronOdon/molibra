2 · Register the asset

⛔⛔ Signing this makes you WSRO-on-Molibra's header authority, permanently. Every claim will rest on Ethereum headers you attest, and no later transaction changes that. The asset id is derived from (chain 1, contract), so it cannot be squatted — but the authority slot is claimed once, by whoever signs first.

2b · Burn the WSRO on Ethereum

⛔⛔ A burn is irreversible. The units are destroyed on Ethereum; they come back only as the Molibra asset, by the claim below.
⛔⛔ Irreversible, and uniquely so. Ownership of the Coinspirit contract is renounced — owner() is the zero address — so mint is permanently unreachable. Burned WSRO can never be re-created, by you or anyone. Bridging is one-way and always will be.

⭐ The upside of the same fact: 21,000,000 is now a ceiling. What remains on Ethereum plus what exists on Molibra is conserved by arithmetic, not by a promise.
